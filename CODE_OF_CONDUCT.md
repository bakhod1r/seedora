# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Most
  disagreements here are about a trade-off someone else has already thought
  about.
- Critique the work: the design, the code, the trade-off. Not the author.
- Take a "no" without escalating, and give one with a reason.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language. Read
  for intent.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning remarks about anyone's background or
identity, deliberate derailing, sustained pressure after a decision has been
made, and publishing anyone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report it to the maintainers privately, through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/bakhod1r/seedora/security/advisories/new)
if it involves anything you would rather not write in public, or by email to
the address in the commit history.

Reports are handled privately. What happens next depends on what happened: a
private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent one. The
reporter is told the outcome. Maintainers are held to the same standard as
everyone else, and a maintainer who is the subject of a report is not the one
who handles it.
